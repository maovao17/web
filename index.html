Payment Landscape

Mobile money is the default, cash still matters

Malawi's payment mix is concentrated around two mobile wallets, with bank rails serving higher-value and corporate transactions. Cash-based collection remains relevant outside urban centers, so a resilient checkout supports both digital and physical channels.
